# Uncap Joins Starknet's BTCFi Season: Earn rewards for using Bitcoin

![BTCFi Season Announcement](/img/BTCFI_Announcement.png)

We're thrilled to announce that Uncap is participating in Starknet's BTCFi Season! 

Starting from November 14 2025, all users depositing BTC and borrowing USDU on Uncap will be eligible to receive additional incentives on top of the native protocol rewards.

<!--truncate-->

## What is BTCFi Season?

The Starknet Foundation has launched an ambitious initiative to position Starknet at the center of BTCFi, backed by a substantial budget of 100M STRK tokens. 

This program aims to make Starknet the most sustainable source of yield on Bitcoin and one of the most cost-effective places to borrow against BTC. 

Learn more about the BTCFi Season in the official [Starknet Foundation announcement](https://www.starknet.io/blog/starknet-foundation-introduces-btcfi-season/)

## Eligibility & Rewards

Uncap users can earn rewards through two simple actions:

**Deposit BTC:** Users who deposit Bitcoin as collateral in Uncap will earn a yield in STRK tokens.

**Borrow USDU:** Users who mint USDU against their Bitcoin collateral will receive a rebate on their borrowing rate, paid in STRK tokens. This effectively makes Uncap the cheapest place to borrow against Bitcoin.

Note that the exact amount of STRK incentives may vary over time based on program parameters and overall participation.

## Distribution

Every Friday, users can claim their accumulated STRK rewards directly through the Uncap interface. No complex procedures, no external platforms - just visit Uncap and claim your weekly earnings.

## Duration

The BTCFi Season incentives will run for a minimum of 6 months starting in October 2025, giving users substantial time to benefit from this program. This represents one of the most significant reward opportunities in the BTCFi ecosystem.

Uncap is positioning itself as the liquidity engine for Bitcoin finance on Starknet. By combining our BTC-backed stablecoin USDU with Starknet's BTCFi incentives, we're creating unprecedented opportunities for Bitcoin holders to put their assets to work.
