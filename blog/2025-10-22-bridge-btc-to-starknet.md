# How to Bridge Bitcoin to Starknet

Getting your Bitcoin onto Starknet is the first step to accessing BTCFi opportunities like Uncap. Here's a straightforward guide to bridging BTC to Starknet using different Bitcoin variants.

<!--truncate-->

## Set Up Your Starknet Wallet

Before bridging, you'll need a Starknet wallet. We recommend [Ready Wallet](https://www.ready.co/ready-wallet) or [Xverse](https://www.xverse.app/) for the best experience.

## Bridging Options

### From Ethereum or L2s

**For most users:** Use [rhino.fi](https://app.rhino.fi/bridge)

**For large amounts:** [StarkGate](https://starkgate.starknet.io/ethereum/bridge?mode=deposit) is recommended, especially for bridging significant volumes from Ethereum

![StarkGate Bridge](/img/starkgate.png)

### From Bitcoin

- **WBTC:** Use [Atomiq](https://atomiq.exchange) or [Garden Finance](https://garden.finance)
- **LBTC:** Visit [Lombard](https://www.lombard.finance/app/stake)
- **TBTC:** Use [Threshold](https://dashboard.threshold.network/tBTC/mint)

### From a Centralized Exchange

You can bridge BTC directly from a centralized exchange using [Layerswap](https://layerswap.io/app?from=BITCOIN_MAINNET&to=STARKNET_MAINNET&fromExchange=BINANCE&fromAsset=BTC&toAsset=WBTC)

## Swapping Between Assets

Once your assets are on Starknet, use [AVNU](https://app.avnu.fi/en) to swap between different tokens and BTC variants.

## Gas Fees on Starknet

One of Starknet's key advantages is its flexible gas payment system. Unlike other chains where you need native tokens for gas, Starknet allows you to pay transaction fees in whatever tokens you have in your wallet. 

This means you can bridge your BTC and start transacting immediately without needing to acquire STRK tokens first.
